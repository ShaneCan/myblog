I am a research associate at the [Chair of Software Technology (ST group)](https://tu-dresden.de/ing/informatik/smt/st), Technische Universität Dresden, where I am pursuing my PhD under the supervision of [Prof. Uwe Aßmann](http://www1.inf.tu-dresden.de/~ua1/) and [Prof. Hartmut Fricke](https://tu-dresden.de/bu/verkehr/ila/ifl/die-professur/staff/Hartmut_Fricke) as part of the [RTG 2947 "AirMetro"](https://tu-dresden.de/bu/verkehr/airmetro/rtg-2947-ueberblick). I am also a visiting researcher at the University of Glasgow, working under [Dr. Michele Sevegnani](https://www.dcs.gla.ac.uk/~michele/index.html).

My current research focuses on integrating digital twins with formal models for Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) operations. My research interests also include computer vision for airport apron monitoring, as well as unmanned vehicle operations on airport surfaces.
